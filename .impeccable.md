## Design Context

### Users

Parkour and freerunning viewers discovering and watching films, documentaries,
and series. Phones use touch; Android TV and Google TV use a remote. Browsing
must remain available without an account.
Android TV is the primary product target. Desktop (Windows, Linux, macOS) follows
the same TV browsing direction using Compose Multiplatform, with keyboard/remote
focus and fullscreen. Keep the phone app supported; iOS/tvOS are out of scope.

### Brand Personality

Calm, cinematic, community-minded. Keep creators visible and official sources intact.

### Aesthetic Direction

The user explicitly requested the Nuvio app as the UI reference on 2026-11-01.
Use large artwork, a cinematic hero, horizontal rows, quiet dark surfaces,
and strong remote focus. Keep JUMPFLIX branding and red accents. Use Noto Sans
to follow the Nuvio reference. Phone and TV layouts share a visual language
while adapting navigation and density to their input devices.

The user's 2026-11-01 correction requires the actual website branding, including
the original popcorn character and Bebas Neue wordmark. Use the supplied character
artwork in the header, launcher icon, and empty states; a generic JF monogram is
not a replacement for those assets.

Use 2:3 portrait posters for browsing cards on both phone and TV, including home
rows, search, and My list. Featured and detail artwork can stay wide.

### Design Principles

- Films and credited creators take precedence over interface decoration.
- Hide catalog-unavailable titles across browsing and search, and hide episodes
  without sources and empty seasons. Keep saved/history data and paid titles.
- Every TV action must be reachable and visibly focused with a D-pad.
- TV keeps the popcorn character in the navigation menu; Home/About use the
  wordmark without a second character. Restore focus to the active menu entry
  when returning from content or a different destination.
- Keep catalog refresh, cached data, and device-local personal data distinguishable.
- Use official provider playback and preserve paid access boundaries.
- Never imply physical-device validation from a build or emulator result.

### Native interaction direction

- Keep the phone masthead a stable height while account/XPOP data loads.
- Home keeps saved-catalog status out of the browsing feed. Featured films rotate
  daily; Continue watching, New, and Explore appear in
  that order. Explore has a Randomize action that reshuffles and resets its row.
- Featured picks exclude completed account watches across providers. Keep unknown
  account history distinct from an empty history; never fill an exhausted unseen
  selection with films already watched.
- Watched browsing posters use a small checkmark and Watched label over a dark
  surface, with an accessible watched state. Keep artwork and remote focus visible;
  do not show a stale resume bar or treat one episode as a completed series.
- The phone masthead is compact (48dp), with a smaller real wordmark/mascot and
  the XPOP kernel plus number, without a trailing XPOP label. People profiles
  replace that masthead with a fixed Back icon and profile name.
- Use portrait posters for browsing and wide video artwork for featured/detail
  backdrops where available. Preserve the supplied poster as the fallback.
- Reserve filled red actions for the main next step; secondary actions use quieter
  outlined surfaces. Keep controls at least 48dp high and TV focus clearly visible.
- Use pull-to-refresh for stats and account data, including TV Up at the top and
  an accessibility refresh action. Do not add standalone stats refresh buttons.
- Native navigation has Home, Search, My list, and Account; About lives under
  Account on phones. The native map view has been removed.
- Creator/athlete names open native profiles with real catalog credits and existing
  public social links. Use initials when no profile portrait exists; do not invent
  portraits or biographies. People search shares Search with film discovery.
- Public reviews stay visible on title details for guests and signed-in viewers.
  Keep reading separate from account-only rating/review composition, and distinguish
  an empty review list from a failed request.
- Player controls respect display cutouts and gesture areas in both orientations.
  Hide idle controls during playback for native streams and phone embeds. The first
  tap or D-pad press reveals them; preserve paused, scrubbing, menu, and accessible
  touch exploration interactions. Keep the video visible as its chrome collapses.
  Use a brief 100 ms fade and a two-second idle timeout after interaction; honor
  Android's animation-off setting and allow input to interrupt hiding immediately.
  Volume, fullscreen, and list saving use icons with accessible action labels.
  A watched film offers Rewatch from zero; an unfinished unwatched film offers Resume.
- Film search includes credited song titles and artists through the cached public
  soundtrack index; People mode remains a name search.
- Series show one selected season above a compact numbered episode list, before
  reviews and account activity. Play/resume is the row's main action; watched status
  stays visible and extra credit/watch actions expand on demand. Preserve custom
  season names and clear touch/remote focus.
