## Design Context

### Users

Parkour and freerunning viewers discovering and watching films, documentaries,
and series. Phones use touch; Android TV and Google TV use a remote. Browsing
must remain available without an account.

### Brand Personality

Calm, cinematic, community-minded. Keep creators visible and official sources intact.

### Aesthetic Direction

The user explicitly requested the Nuvio app as the UI reference on 2026-11-01.
Use large artwork, a cinematic hero, horizontal rows, quiet dark surfaces,
and strong remote focus. Keep JUMPFLIX branding and red accents. Use Noto Sans
to follow the Nuvio reference. Phone and TV layouts share a visual language
while adapting navigation and density to their input devices.

The user's 2026-11-01 correction requires the actual website branding, including
the original popcorn character and Bebas Neue wordmark. Use the supplied character
artwork in the header, launcher icon, and empty states; a generic JF monogram is
not a replacement for those assets.

Use 2:3 portrait posters for browsing cards on both phone and TV, including home
rows, search, and My list. Featured and detail artwork can stay wide.

### Design Principles

- Films and credited creators take precedence over interface decoration.
- Every TV action must be reachable and visibly focused with a D-pad.
- Keep catalog refresh, cached data, and device-local personal data distinguishable.
- Use official provider playback and preserve paid access boundaries.
- Never imply physical-device validation from a build or emulator result.

### Native interaction direction

- Keep the phone masthead a stable height while account/XPOP data loads.
- Use portrait posters for browsing and wide video artwork for featured/detail
  backdrops where available. Preserve the supplied poster as the fallback.
- Reserve filled red actions for the main next step; secondary actions use quieter
  outlined surfaces. Keep controls at least 48dp high and TV focus clearly visible.
- Use pull-to-refresh for stats and account data, including TV Up at the top and
  an accessibility refresh action. Do not add standalone stats refresh buttons.
- Native navigation has Home, Search, My list, and Account; About lives under
  Account on phones. The native map view has been removed.
- Creator/athlete names open native profiles with real catalog credits and existing
  public social links. Use initials when no profile portrait exists; do not invent
  portraits or biographies. People search shares Search with film discovery.
